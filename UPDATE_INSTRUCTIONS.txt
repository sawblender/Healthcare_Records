📋 INSTRUCTIONS TO ADD EDIT & DELETE FUNCTIONALITY
================================================

STEP 1: Update Smart Contract in Remix IDE
-------------------------------------------
1. Open Remix IDE (https://remix.ethereum.org)
2. Copy the content from: blockchain/Healthcare_Updated.sol
3. Compile the contract (Solidity Compiler 0.8.19+)
4. Deploy to Ganache:
   - Environment: "Injected Provider - MetaMask"
   - Make sure MetaMask is connected to Ganache (localhost:7545)
   - Deploy with your admin account
5. Copy the new contract address

STEP 2: Update ABI in Frontend
-------------------------------
1. After deployment in Remix, copy the ABI:
   - Go to "Solidity Compiler" tab
   - Click "ABI" button (copy to clipboard)
2. Replace content in: frontend/src/HealthcareABI.json
3. Update contract address in these files:
   - frontend/src/AdminLogin.js (line 5)
   - frontend/src/DoctorLogin.js (line 5)
   - frontend/src/PatientLogin.js (line 5)

STEP 3: Test the Features
--------------------------
